# Session 0007 — Phase 6, the four open interaction items

**Scope:** `docs/phase-6-worklist.md`, written 2026-08-05 after a manual pass with the
user. Four items, all found by using the pane rather than by reading it. This session is
mid-phase: it is not the whole of Phase 6, and §2 says plainly which of the phase's exit
criteria it moved and which it did not touch.

## 1. Outcome

A drag on a ghostty pane now selects the same characters cascadia would, from the same
pixels. That was four separate defects wearing one symptom, and the fix for the largest
one came out of measurement rather than reasoning — two earlier attempts at it had been
made and reverted.

- **The press was in the wrong place.** Windows Terminal hands the core selection
  *boundaries*, and the anchor arrives from `SetSelectionAnchor` on the first drag
  movement — after the direction is known. `LeftClickOnTerminal` fires before that with
  the click position *rounded*, and ghostty latches its anchor on a press and will not
  re-latch. So the anchor was a character late whenever the click landed in the right half
  of a character. A single click now presses nothing at all; the press moved to the anchor.
- **`clear_selection` exists as a binding action** (ghostty patch 28) and ends the click
  gesture along with the selection. `ClearSelection` no longer imitates a click, and the
  anchor re-press has something that can break a click sequence — without it, a press
  within one cell width of the last is a double click and selects by word.
- **Settings cross as a key and a value** (`ghostty_config_set_kv`, patch 25 amended), and
  neither is parsed. The quoting and escaping in the translator are gone. WT's default
  word delimiters now reach the engine intact: a double click on `Corporation.` comes back
  `Corporation`, as it does on cascadia.
- **A triple click highlights the whole line** (`selection-trim-line`, patch 29) and still
  copies only the line's content.

Everything above is covered by a check that runs unattended, and the two that could only
be answered by the real engine live in `scripts/smoke-harness.ps1`, which grew from three
checks to six.

**By hand, in a ghostty pane beside a cascadia one:**

1. Press in the *right half* of a character and drag right. Both panes must start the
   selection on the character you pressed on. This was the reported bug.
2. Drag right to left. The character you started on stays selected.
3. Triple click a short line. Both highlights reach the right edge of the pane. Paste:
   neither has trailing spaces.
4. Double click `Corporation.` in some output. Both select `Corporation`.
5. Right-click to clear a selection, then drag a new one: the new drag must select by
   character, not by word.

## 4. Deviations

1. **The worklist's order (3 → 1 → 4 → 2) became 4 → 3 → 1 → 2.** Item 3 was ordered
   first because it was believed to be WT-side only and to need no rebuild. Measuring it
   showed otherwise: the fix requires re-pressing at the corrected anchor, and ghostty
   counts a press within one cell width of the previous one as a double click, so
   something has to end the click sequence first. That something is item 4's
   `clear_selection`. Item 3 is still almost entirely WT-side, but it depends on item 4's
   patch and therefore on a libghostty rebuild.

2. **Item 2 is a config option, not a change of ghostty's default.** The worklist says
   "change ghostty's line selection to span the full row". Doing that literally would
   change triple-click for every ghostty user and would not survive upstreaming (ADR 0004
   / Phase 9). `selection-trim-line` defaults to ghostty's existing behaviour and WT sets
   it false; the WT pane behaves exactly as asked. Flagged rather than assumed because it
   is a deliberate reading of the instruction.

3. **`ghostty_config_set` was replaced rather than joined by `ghostty_config_set_kv`.**
   The worklist says "add". Keeping the line form would have kept the footgun with no
   caller, so patch 25 was amended to have one entry point. The harness moved with it.

4. **A finding that was not in the worklist: `_mouseTo` has no padding term, and that is
   load-bearing.** ghostty subtracts the surface's `padding_left` before deciding which
   side of a cell the pointer is on. WT pins `window-padding-x/y` to zero, so the term is
   zero *by configuration*. The harness was running with ghostty's default padding and was
   therefore measuring a geometry nobody ships; it now pins padding to zero too. Both
   facts are commented at the code and pinned by
   `GhosttySettingsTests::PaddingIsZeroBecauseWindowsTerminalAlreadyPads`.

5. **`terminal.selection_codepoints` had to be exported from `src/terminal/main.zig`.**
   `zig build test` compiles a module set where it was reachable and the `-Dapp-runtime=none`
   library build does not — so the tests passed while the DLL failed to build. Worth
   knowing: **`zig build test` passing is not sufficient evidence that libghostty builds.**

## 5. Decisions needed

None. Everything above was decidable inside the phase's envelope; the two judgment calls
(§4.2, §4.3) are recorded as deviations rather than escalated because both keep the
delivered behaviour exactly as the worklist specified.

## 6. Learnings for the plan

- **Both engines are boundary models; they differ only in how a pixel becomes a
  boundary.** Written up in `docs/selection-geometry.md` with both rules measured against
  their running engines. cascadia: the pressed cell is always selected whole (the press
  fraction is *irrelevant* — the drag path re-anchors and discards the press site's
  rounding) and the far end moves at the midpoint. ghostty: one 60%-of-cell threshold at
  both ends. This is the document to read before touching selection again.
- **The press site's `std::round` looks load-bearing and is not.** It was the thing that
  misled two earlier attempts. It is now stated as its own test
  (`SelectionDragGeometryTests::PressPositionWithinTheCellDoesNotMoveTheAnchor`) so the
  next reader is not misled the same way.
- **An entry point that takes an encoded string pushes the encoding onto every caller,
  and getting it wrong is silent.** The word-delimiter bug parsed successfully and
  reported nothing. Diagnostics do not surface at all under `-Dapp-runtime=none`, so "no
  error was logged" is worth nothing as evidence in this project. Prefer entry points that
  take values, not text.
- **Two kinds of escaping were being conflated.** The entry format's, which is now gone,
  and a *field's* own — `selection-word-chars` is parsed as a Zig string literal, so its
  backslashes still need doubling. Both are commented where they live.
- **Proposal for Phase 7/9:** `ghostty_text_s` carries a top-left pixel and offsets but no
  bottom-right, so an embedder cannot read a selection's extent. The triple-click check
  works around it with `offset_len`. Worth a small upstream addition.

## 7. Next-phase readiness gaps

This phase is not finished; the gaps are within it.

- **Search is the largest remaining item and is entirely unstarted.** The readiness gate
  answered how to drive it (binding actions; asynchronous `SEARCH_TOTAL`/`SEARCH_SELECTED`
  against WT's synchronous `SearchResults`) but nothing is built. The asynchronous/
  synchronous mismatch is the risk and deserves its own design note before implementation.
- **IME is unstarted, and `CursorPosition` returning `{}` will place the candidate window
  wrong** before anything else is even tried. Start there.
- **HTML copy is wired and never verified.** It cannot be checked from a unit test and
  cannot be checked from the harness without a clipboard reader; the honest options are a
  manual checklist item or a small addition to the harness.
- **Keyboard/quick-edit selection was assumed to be covered by the "marks are prompt-level
  only" decision and is not.** It needs its own answer before the phase's first criterion
  can be called met.
- **No session report exists for the earlier Phase 6 work** (selection, copy, clipboard,
  `ReadEntireBuffer`, `scrollToMark` are all committed in `terminal/` with no report in
  `docs/sessions/`). The phase's history has a hole in it, and the retro should decide
  whether to reconstruct it.
